Data Deletion Instructions - gta6viral

The "gta6viral" app does not store personal data about Page visitors, followers or other users. The only credential it holds is the access token of the Page owner, kept on the owner's own computer.

To remove the app's access to your Facebook account

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  3. Find gta6viral and click Remove.
  4. Choose to delete the posts and activity the app made, if you wish.

Removing the app revokes its access token immediately.

To request deletion of any other data

Open an issue at github.com/joaowick/gtavi-claudecode/issues describing your request. Requests are handled on a best-effort basis.
