Privacy Policy - gta6viral

Last updated: 2026-11-22

What this app is

"gta6viral" is a private publishing tool operated by its owner to schedule and publish short videos to the owner's own Facebook Page (GTA6Viral). It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner uses it to log in.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, track or store personal data from visitors, followers or users of the Page. It only uses the Facebook permissions granted by the Page owner (for example, to list the owner's Pages and to publish videos to them). The Facebook access token issued to the owner is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to publish the owner's content to the owner's Page. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Videos and hosting

Videos prepared for publishing may be temporarily hosted on a public storage bucket so that Facebook can download them. They contain no personal data of Page visitors. Content published to the Page is governed by Facebook's own terms and privacy policy.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no personal data about other people, there is nothing further to delete. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites) → remove "gta6viral". See also the data deletion instructions.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/joaowick/gtavi-claudecode/issues.

Política de Privacidade (resumo em português)

O "gta6viral" é uma ferramenta privada, usada pelo proprietário para agendar e publicar vídeos curtos na própria Página do Facebook (GTA6Viral). O app não coleta, rastreia nem armazena dados pessoais de visitantes ou seguidores da Página, não vende nem compartilha dados com terceiros. O token de acesso fica apenas no computador do proprietário. Para remover o acesso do app, use as Integrações de Negócios nas configurações do Facebook. Contato: página de Issues do repositório acima.
